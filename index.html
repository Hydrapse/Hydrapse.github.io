About Me

I am a 4th-year Ph.D. candidate (estimate graduation 2027 summer) in Computer Science at University of Southern California (USC), advised by Prof. Viktor Prasanna. Before that, I got my B.S. degree in Software Engineering from Central South University (CSU), advised by Prof. Ning Gui. My current research interests lie in AI coding and LLM reasoning over graph-structured data.
